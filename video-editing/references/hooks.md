# Hooks (the first 1-3 seconds)

The hook decides whether anyone watches. One video = one hook = one promise that the video then keeps.

## Hook patterns that work

1. **Result first**: show the end result before explaining. "This is what 30 days of editing daily did to my videos."
2. **Direct question**: aim it at the viewer's problem. "بتعمل مونتاج ساعات ولسه الفيديو مالوش روح؟"
3. **Bold claim + proof**: "I cut this 40-minute video to 60 seconds. Watch how."
4. **Pattern break**: start mid-action, mid-sentence, or with an unexpected visual. No intro, no logo, no "أهلاً بيكم".
5. **Callout**: name exactly who it is for. "لو أنت بتصور بموبايلك، الفيديو ده ليك."
6. **Mistake confession**: "غلطة وحشة كنت بعملها في الترجمة بتاعتي."

## Rules

- Never start with a greeting, channel intro, or logo animation. Cut them all.
- The hook text on screen should repeat or tighten the spoken hook; many people watch muted first.
- Say the hook in under 3 seconds. If it takes longer, split it: visual hook (text/action) + spoken hook.
- Write 3 hook options for every video and pick the most specific one. Specific beats clever.
- The first cut of the video must happen within the first 2 seconds.

## Arabic / Egyptian notes

- Dialect beats formal Arabic for hooks on TikTok/Reels. "إزاي تعمل كذا" outperforms "كيف تقوم بكذا" with Egyptian audiences.
- Keep hook text short: 3-6 words on screen, because Arabic reads slower at small sizes.
- Common Egyptian hook openers: "محدش قالك إن...", "لو لسه بتعمل...", "الحاجة الوحيدة اللي غيرت...", "جربت الطريقة دي عشانك".
