# Shot Lists & Scripts

A shot list turns "I want to make a video about X" into something filmable. Fill the brackets with the user's content.

## Talking-head tutorial (20-40s)

1. HOOK (0-3s): close-up, say the result or problem. On-screen text: [hook, 3-6 words]
2. STEP 1 (3-12s): show the action on screen/hands while explaining. Text: [step name]
3. STEP 2 (12-20s): same pattern.
4. STEP 3 (20-28s): same pattern.
5. RESULT (28-33s): show the before/after.
6. CTA (33-36s): "تابعني لو عايز جزء تاني" / "Follow for part 2".

## Voiceover + b-roll (no face on camera)

1. HOOK: strongest b-roll shot + voiceover hook line.
2. 3-6 b-roll clips, each 2-4s, matched to what the voiceover says at that moment.
3. Captions on everything (many watch muted).
4. CTA on final shot.

## Filming rules for beginners (phone)

- Clean the lens. Film in daylight facing a window; never with the window behind you.
- Film vertical (9:16) if the video is for TikTok/Reels/Shorts; decide before filming, not in the edit.
- Record 5 seconds before and after each take; handles make cutting easier.
- Audio matters more than video: film in a quiet room, phone 30-50 cm from the mouth. A blanket over a table kills echo.
- Film the hook last, once you know the video's strongest point.

## Script format

Two columns: what is SAID | what is SEEN. One row per shot. Write the spoken lines word-for-word for hooks and CTAs; bullet points are fine for the middle.
