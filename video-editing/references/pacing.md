# Pacing & Cutting

Attention is the whole game. Every second must earn the next one.

## Cut ruthlessly

- Delete every silence longer than ~0.5 seconds in talking videos (jump cut or use CapCut's "Remove filler/silence").
- Delete repeated information. If two clips say the same thing, keep the stronger one.
- Delete the warm-up. Most recordings only get good after the first 10 seconds; start there.

## Rhythm

- Talking head: cut every 2-4 seconds (jump cut, zoom punch, or angle change). A static shot longer than 5 seconds loses viewers.
- Tutorials: one step per shot. Show the action while saying it, never before or after.
- B-roll: cover cuts and illustrate claims. Film 3-5 extra shots per video (hands, screen close-up, wide shot, detail).
- Pattern interrupt every 5-8 seconds: zoom, angle switch, text pop-up, sound effect, or b-roll. One is enough; stacking them looks chaotic.

## Structure templates (short-form)

- **Listicle**: hook → item 1 (5-8s) → item 2 → item 3 → recap + CTA.
- **Tutorial**: hook (the result) → step 1 → step 2 → step 3 → result again → CTA.
- **Story**: hook (the moment of tension) → context (fast) → what happened → what I learned.

## Retention checks

- Watch the edit once with sound off: does it still make sense? If not, captions or visuals are missing.
- Watch at 2x speed: anywhere you get bored, cut.
- The last 2 seconds should point somewhere: follow, comment prompt, or part 2.
